1. Introduction & Overview

Creami Cravings ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our web application, Progressive Web App (PWA), and Android Trusted Web Activity (TWA) collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

Creami Cravings collects only minimal data necessary to provide a personalized, responsive recipe companion experience:

  • Local Kitchen Data (Device-Only): Your pantry inventory, ingredient in-stock toggles, freezer pints tracker, grocery shopping lists, and active ingredient substitutions are stored primarily inside your browser's local storage (localStorage) and Cache Storage.
  • Google Account Information (Optional): When you choose to authenticate using Google Sign-In, we receive your verified Google account email address, display name, and avatar picture provided by Google Identity Services. This allows you to securely sync your personal recipes, favorites, and freezer inventory across multiple devices.
  • Tasting Notes & Ratings: Recipe ratings (1 to 5 stars), personal custom recipes, and tasting notes you submit are associated with your account or stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Matching the ingredients currently in your pantry against the 243+ Ninja Creami cookbook recipes.
  • Tracking the 16-hour freezing countdown and triggering native notifications when your pints are ready to spin.
  • Syncing your custom recipes, favorite bookmarks, and made batch counts to your Google account when signed in.
  • Calculating community aggregate rating averages without disclosing private personal tasting notes.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. Creami Cravings contains no third-party ad networks, no tracking beacons, no data brokers, and no analytics fingerprinting scripts.

5. Device Notifications & Service Worker

Creami Cravings uses a background Service Worker to provide 100% offline access and schedule optional 16-hour freeze timer alerts. Notification permission is requested exclusively when you choose to enable freeze alerts, and you may revoke this permission at any time directly through your browser or Android system settings.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Local Data: You may clear your pantry and local storage at any time through your browser's "Clear Site Data" settings or by utilizing our local Data Backup/Reset tool.
  • Cloud Account Deletion: If you signed in with Google, you may request complete deletion of your account and all associated personal data by contacting our administrator at admin@creamicravings.com or using the account management portal.

7. Children's Privacy

Creami Cravings is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
